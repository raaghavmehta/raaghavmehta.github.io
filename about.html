Biography

The Story So Far

My journey has been shaped by a curiosity for sound, technology and the experiences we create through them.
It began with a small sparked interest in multimedia and technology, which gradually found its way into the world of music production, and sound design.
Over time, that interest has expanded into the broader fields of broadcast engineering, live production and professional audio.
Over the years, I've had the opportunity to work across newsrooms, live broadcasts, media infrastructure and cloud-based streaming environments, contributing to the systems and workflows that bring content to audiences around the world.
Alongside the technical side, my interest in sound has remained a constant — from music production and field recordings to Foley, sound design and immersive audio experiences.
Today, I find myself somewhere at the intersection of these disciplines: exploring how technology, sound and storytelling can come together to create meaningful experiences.
This is an evolving collection of the work, experiments, ideas and experiences that have shaped that journey so far.
